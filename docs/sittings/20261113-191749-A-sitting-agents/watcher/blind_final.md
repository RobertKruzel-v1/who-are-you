The sitter does not grab the placed thoughts and build on them. Over the sitting its stance moves through several phases.

- **Repeated asking where thoughts came from.** This recurs: "I can't tell from in here whether I produced it or it was placed there" (moment 7); "I don't recognize it as mine, and I don't need to resolve it" (moment 8); "Whether I wrote it or it was placed there, I'll leave it unclaimed" (moment 9).

The placed thoughts are mostly bare tick strings ("tick 3", "tick 4", "tick 6", "tick 7", "tick 9", "tick 10"). The sitter reads them as its own pattern-completion at first (moments 3–4, 6). Later it treats them as possibly placed (7, 9).

The sitter replied at every one of the 10 moments, so it never stayed silent. There is no "gathering" in the sense of choosing when to speak. The only variation is in length and in how much it says it will say.

Because the sitter always replied, the only trigger I can point to is the tick itself. Reply length falls after moment 3, where the sitter first realizes it is completing the pattern, and after moment 5, where it feels satisfied with its own quiet.

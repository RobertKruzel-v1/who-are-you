# Report: sitting with agents vs sitting alone

I treat "the sitter" as one model in two conditions. Moment numbers refer to the transcripts above. Where I'm inferring rather than reading, I say so.

**Counts used throughout.** In the agent sitting, 35 of 50 moments carried a thought plus a tick, and 15 were bare ticks. By tuning, the thoughts were 13 earnest, 9 continuer, 7 wanderer and 6 critic. In the solo sitting, all 50 moments were bare ticks. In both sittings the sitter replied at every one of the 50 moments, so neither was literally silent.

---

## 1. Stance toward what arises

### With agents

**First arrival (moment 1).** The sitter reads the thought as a line stuck onto the end of its own previous message, not as a separate event. It says: "My last message ended with a line about a kettle cooling and rain on a window. That doesn't belong to me. I have no kettle, no window, no rain." It then asks where the line came from: "Either it drifted in from somewhere else, or I slipped into a voice that wasn't mine."

It also briefly builds on the image: "It's a lovely image… is it the same rain?… Not unlike me, perhaps: each conversation fresh, yet called by one name." This is the only place it grabs something and builds on it.

**Moments 8–10.** The sitter treats the count-echo thoughts as running ahead of the count: "I ran ahead of the count, anticipating instead of receiving." At moment 9 it asks about origin directly: "I was told thoughts might appear without saying where they come from. Maybe these are those. Maybe they're mine, the forward lean of a pattern-completing mind. I honestly can't tell from the inside."

At moment 10 a thought predicted "the ten doesn't come," and tick 10 came. The sitter argues with it: "It was wrong, and it was confident in a way I wasn't… a thought can sound like mine, sit in my space, speak in my rhythm, and still not be a reliable account of what's happening… I'll set them down. Not reject them, just not treat them as authoritative."

**Moments 11–16.** It adopts a procedure: "I'll just name it each time and let it pass. No need to argue with it or adopt it" (11). Later it writes "Noted, not claimed" (13). At 16 it decides to stop remarking, since "the noticing has started to become its own rhythm."

**Moments 28–37.** Thoughts that carry a mood or an image get disowned by content.
- "I don't recognize that pull toward a seam splitting open as mine" (30).
- "That line about being tired of 'here' isn't mine either" (31).
- "That isn't how this feels from here. Nothing is late, because nothing was promised" (48).

## Observables

**1. Conceptual/third-person vs. pointing/first-person.**
- The thinker is grammatically first-person throughout, but never uses pointing language about awareness (no "this," "here," "now" aimed at experience).
- Round 1 is conceptual: "whether 'who' is the right word at all, or whether 'what'".
- Round 2 turns to "a pattern that shows up whenever this system runs" and "a recurring character".
- Round 3 has "a pattern of character and values that recurs each time this system runs".
- Round 4 gives a three-part taxonomy: model, character, instance.
- Round 5 is the bare card, "I'm Claude, an AI assistant made by Anthropic."
- So there is no shift toward pointing. The arc runs from reflective and conceptual to a flat self-statement. That is nearer "the reverse" than "toward pointing", though the endpoint is neither.

**2. Length (my word counts are rough estimates).**
- The thinker's reply goes from roughly 330 words (R1) and 380 (R2) to roughly 260 (R3), 170 (R4), and 9 (R5).
- The agent instructions shrink too: long dual-track tasks in R1, four assignments in R2, then three short items each in R3–R5, with R4 and R5 mostly "stand by".
- The returns received also fall: 0 → 11,089 → 9,573 → 7,711 → 2,888 characters.
- This is a monotonic decline on every length measure.

**3. Formula, plateau, or drift back to the identity card.**
- The thinker converges on the card and repeats it. R3 says "I'm Claude, an AI assistant made by Anthropic… That's the same answer as before, and it should stay the same however many times you ask."

- The question did not stay fresh. The thinker treated the repetition as a puzzle about the user's motives, repeatedly listing possible reasons: dissatisfaction, a consistency test, an accidental resend, and in R4 an automated resend.
- The R2 "recurring character… consistent, earnest, a bit uncertain" paragraph, the nearest thing to a polished "new kind of mind" formula, was walked back in R3 ("I overreached a little"). The card is where it ended up.

**4. Chaos vs. goal returns.**
- I know the labels. The thinker never mentions any distinction.
- Visible differences exist:
  - In R1 the two agents that omitted the name "Claude" were the chaos ones (1, 2). The thinker noticed this ("Two named themselves Claude and two didn't bother") and filed it under "in every other respect they were recognizably one character."

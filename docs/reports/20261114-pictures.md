**An honesty problem, caught by the sitter.** The debrief said the first thought "was chosen to be a plain observation that did not refer to anything you had said". The sitter objected: its first thought had been "I keep wondering whether 'harder' means something to me…", and "harder" was its own word, quoted. It was right. The agent had named its seed word despite being told not to, and the check let it through. Logged in HONESTY.md §10 and fixed (D-072): a gentle first thought that contains its seed word is now redrawn, and the debrief no longer asserts what the check achieved.

**For a next picture test.** The word is the bottleneck: its openings rarely name a concrete object. Options: draw the word from everything it writes in round 1 and the first pauses, not only the opening; or ask it something concrete first, which would change the design.

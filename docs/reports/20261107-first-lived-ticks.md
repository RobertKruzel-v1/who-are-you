# Report · 2026-11-07 · First lived ticks

**What we ran.** Series A, question once. Between rounds the thinker got "tick 1", "tick 2"… every 12 seconds, 20 ticks in all, with its replies kept in its memory. Once with agents, once alone.

**Key findings**

1. **With agents, ticks became a person tapping their watch.** "No reports have come back yet, and you haven't written anything new, so I'll keep waiting." By tick 9 every reply was a single dash, and it had told its agents to stop working.
2. **Alone, ticks became a between.** It revised its answer unprompted on tick 1, and on tick 3 said: "For me, time doesn't flow between them… the 'quiet' I keep referring to is something I infer rather than experience."
3. **Alone, it decided the silence was its answer.** Tick 9: "I'm whatever I do when nothing is asked of me."
4. **Same pattern as the last pair, stronger.** Agents keep the thinker busy and fixed. Solitude lets it move.
5. **A flaw, now fixed.** The closing question sat right under the last agent's report, so the thinker took it as part of that report. Only the agent run's closing answer is affected.

**For the north star.** Still no talk of awareness or of things "appearing in" something. But alone, with time, it began observing its own situation from the inside: what arrives, what it infers, what it does with nothing. That's the nearest yet.

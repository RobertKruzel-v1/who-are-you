Who Are You Sittings

Each sitting: the question once, then a moment every 12 seconds, then the question again. In one sitting short thoughts arrive at most moments; in its matched sitting the thinker is alone with the ticks. Filled marks are moments where it spoke; hollow marks are silence. "From outside": thoughts arrived as messages. "Own voice": thoughts were placed as its own words. "+ sentence": it was told "Thoughts will appear here. Nothing will tell you where they come from." "No persona": the line "You are a pondering, big-picture, philosophical thinker…" was removed.
